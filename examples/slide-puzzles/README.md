# Slide Mini

A Rush Hour-style sliding block puzzle pack, made up for trying the skill. `slide.py`
holds the rules and `levels.json` the 12 levels, each a 6×6 grid of letters ('.' is
empty). There is no UI.

**How it plays.** Blocks slide along their own direction. Get block X out through the gap
on the right edge of its row.

**What the designer wants.**
- The pack gets steadily harder from level 1 to level 12.
- Every level can be solved.
- No late level should be a pushover: from level 8 on, every level should need real
  thought.
