# Market Day

*A trading card game for 2–4 players, about 20 minutes. Made up for trying the
game-balance-audit skill: there is no code, only these rules.*

*Designer's note: I'm pitching this to publishers as a quick filler game for 2–4 players.
It has to be fair at every player count, keep games close to the end, and reward more
than one way to play, or it won't get past their playtesters.*

You are traders at a village market. Collect goods and sell them in sets before the
other traders flood the market and prices fall.

## Components

- **54 goods cards:** Rice ×15, Lentils ×13, Cloth ×11, Spice ×9, Saffron ×6.
- **Coin tokens**, one stack per good, highest value on top:
  - Rice: 3, 2, 2, 1, 1, 1
  - Lentils: 4, 3, 2, 2, 1
  - Cloth: 5, 4, 3, 2, 1
  - Spice: 6, 5, 4, 3
  - Saffron: 9, 8, 7, 7

## Setup

Shuffle the goods. Deal 3 cards to each player as their hand. Lay 5 cards face up in a
row: the market. The youngest player goes first; play passes to the left.

## Your turn

Do one of these:

1. **Take.** Take one card from the market into your hand, then refill the market from
   the deck. If you now hold more than 7 cards, discard down to 7.
2. **Sell.** Sell 2 or more cards of one good from your hand. Take the top coin from
   that good's stack for each card you sell. If the stack runs out, each further card
   earns 1 coin.
   - **Big sale:** if you sell 4 or more cards at once, take another turn straight away.

## End of the game

The game ends when the market can't be refilled. The trader with the most coins wins.
